*Lade alle 4 Bilder als Keyframes hoch und nutze diesen Prompt:*

**Prompt:**
> "A seamless, high-velocity cinematic travel montage. Starting from a view out of an airplane window, the white fluffy clouds physically morph and liquefy into underwater bubbles, transitioning smoothly into a vibrant coral reef dive. ### Clip 2: Tauchen (Start) -> Skifahren (Ende)
**Prompt:**
> "Surreal transition. The blue underwater ocean bubbles rise and freeze, morphing into flying white snow powder. The coral reef structure transforms into a rugged mountain slope. Fast camera movement, energetic transition from water to snow."

### Clip 3: Skifahren (Start) -> Alpen/Wandern (Ende)
**Prompt:**
